AI “vibe coding” workflow

From new marketing angle to 40 updated repos.

  1. 01 · Centralize the story.
    I define the new angle (e.g., discipline, degen, underdog, community) as structured data: tone, vocabulary, hook formulas, FAQs, and objection patterns.
  2. 02 · Regenerate content with AI.
    Using Claude / Gemini, I re-spin hero copy, proof sections, FAQs, and meta tags for every brand using your CSV of keywords, verticals, and affiliate data.
  3. 03 · Codegen & templates.
    Static site generator or code scripts turn that structured content into Tailwind-powered pages, using shared components for hero, proof rails, FAQs, and guarantees.
  4. 04 · Multi-repo update.
    A GitHub-driven workflow pulls 30–40 repos, applies the updated templates/content, commits with consistent messages, and pushes changes or opens PRs per repo.
  5. 05 · QA, SEO & speed checks.
    Automated checks (Lighthouse/Pagespeed, link integrity, basic analytics) verify that TikTok traffic lands on fast, legible, and high-intent pages — before scaling the change.
